# Lunar Talisman — English SEO keyword map

## What this document is for

This is the working keyword map for the English-language storefront. It is designed for the Americas, Europe, and Southeast Asia, where English is used in search. It deliberately avoids a `meta keywords` tag: modern search engines do not use it as a ranking signal.

Search demand changes by country, language, season, and ad market. The tiers below are priority bands, not a claim of a fixed global monthly-search number. Before paid acquisition or content production, confirm country-level monthly ranges and keyword difficulty in Google Ads Keyword Planner and Google Search Console.
